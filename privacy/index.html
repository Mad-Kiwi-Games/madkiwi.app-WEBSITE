1. Information We Collect

Automatically collected

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID / IDFA, GAID, IDFV) and device specifications (OS, model, language, timezone)
  • Gameplay and usage data (sessions, progression, in-game events, feature usage)
  • Approximate location, derived from IP address (country / region level only — we do not collect precise GPS location)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completions)
  • Install and attribution data (which campaign or channel led to your install)

Provided by you

  • Information you include when you contact support@madkiwi.app or privacy@madkiwi.app (e.g. your email address and message content)

We do not require account registration to play our games, and we do not knowingly collect your name, physical address, or payment card details — purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google (see Section 4 of our Terms of Service).

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our games
  • To understand how players engage with our games (analytics)
  • To show, measure, and personalize in-game advertising
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues
  • To determine which marketing channel led to an install (attribution)

  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, cheating, or abuse

4. Advertising & Your Choices

Our games are free to play and supported by in-game advertising. Some ads are personalized based on advertising identifiers collected by our ad partners.

  • iOS: You will be asked for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. You may allow or deny tracking at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

Opting out of tracking does not stop ads from showing, but it limits how they are personalized.

5. Data Retention

We retain the data described above only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes — after which it is deleted or anonymized. Support correspondence is retained for a reasonable period to handle follow-up questions.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us at privacy@madkiwi.app and we will delete it.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@madkiwi.app. We aim to respond within 30 days.
